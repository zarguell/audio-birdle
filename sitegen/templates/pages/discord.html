Play in Discord

The same daily bird, inside a server, with friends.

Add it to your server

Installing needs the Manage Server permission. The bot asks for View Channel, Send Messages, Embed Links, and Attach Files — nothing else.

Add Audio-Birdle to Discord

The bot appears offline in the member list. That is normal: it uses HTTP interactions only, with no Gateway connection.

How to play

  1. Open the App Launcher — desktop: the button at the right of the message bar; mobile: the button at the left — and choose Audio-Birdle. The entry point is not in the / command list.
  2. Listen to the bird call and pick the species. A public “<name> is playing” card is posted in the channel and updated with your result.
  3. Friends can join from the card's Play now! button.
  4. Run /audiobirdle-setup in a channel to get that channel's daily summary.

Commands

Command What it does
/audiobirdle-setup Post daily summaries in the current channel (Manage Server)
/audiobirdle-stats Your record and current streak
/audiobirdle-forget Delete your stored Discord data

Groups are per channel. Summaries are sectioned by region and mode, and scores are never compared across regions — different regions are different daily puzzles.

Data and privacy

Playing in Discord requires a Discord identity, so that part is not anonymous. The bot stores your Discord id, username, avatar, and daily results so it can show boards and streaks. See the privacy policy for retention and deletion, and the terms of service.

Audio-Birdle is an independent project and is not affiliated with Discord.
